Rework the solar system: eight fresh planets (Meridian Gate, Solace Yards, Cinder Dock, Vesper Port stations plus Tern, Halcyon, Lumen, Nadir) and four moons (Wisp, Moth, Rill, Tarn) on hierarchical planet-centered orbits. Guidance routes around per-body wells with slingshot gates and delayed departures, the chart flip search dodges wells with waypoint fallbacks and renders moon orbits, and each of the four dockable stations gets a distinct walkable hull, market table, and map identity.
