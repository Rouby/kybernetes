Trade flow screens: overview, bench entry, and per-crate selling

- The market console is now a trade overview (listings with buy/sell
  prices and stock) with two entries: BUY opens the packing bench and
  SELL opens a dedicated sell screen.
- The sell screen lists every bay crate hauled into the hub with its
  payout in green, sealable per crate or all at once.
- Pack palette buttons show the per-press price in red; buttons support
  an optional right-aligned detail span in its own color.
